Marie Marley

PhD — Author & Alzheimer’s Caregiver Advocate

Marie Marley is an award-winning author whose work sits at the intersection of love, loss, and resilience. Drawing on her own decade-long experience as the primary caregiver for her partner, Eduard, she writes with rare candor about the emotional landscape of Alzheimer's caregiving — the grief that arrives before death, the humor that survives everything, and the love that refuses to let go.

Her books have been praised by caregivers, clinicians, and family members across the country as indispensable guides for navigating one of life’s most demanding chapters. She holds a doctorate in human development and has been featured in publications including The Huffington Post, Psychology Today, and The Chicago Tribune.

When she’s not writing, Marie lectures at universities and caregiver conferences, advocates for policy reform in long-term care, and answers letters from readers who find themselves where she once stood — uncertain, exhausted, and still full of love.
