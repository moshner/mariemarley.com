Marie Marley, PhD

Author & Alzheimer’s Caregiver Advocate

Marie Marley was an expert grant writer who, over the years, acquired a keen understanding of many geriatric topics, including dementia. However, none of that could have prepared her for the sometimes-difficult demands of loving and caring for a person with Alzheimer's disease.

For seven years, she was a caregiver for Edward Theodoru, her life partner of 30 years, who had Alzheimer's. After his death in 2007, Marie began writing books that have dementia as either a primary or a secondary theme. These include an award-winning memoir about her relationship with Ed (Come Back Early Today), a “How To” book for Alzheimer's caregivers (Finding Joy in Alzheimer's, with co-author, Daniel Potts, MD, FAAN, a literary novel (The Concerto) and a forthcoming mystery (Silent Witness to Murder).

Marie earned a PhD in Musicology in 1978, then had a successful 30-year career as a medical grant writer. She is retired from the University of Cincinnati Department of Family Medicine and the American Academy of Family Physicians. Marie lives in Kansas City with her darling Shih Tzu, Christina.

Books by Marie Marley

Honest, compassionate, and fiercely human: books that turn caregiving darkness into light.

Come Back Early Today: A Memoir of Love, Alzheimer's and Joy (2011)

Available in paperback and Kindle version

Alzheimer's caregivers who have read the book say it helped them very much. Former caregivers say they wish they'd read it when they were caregivers.

“Come Back Early Today” tells the powerful 30-year love story of a young American woman and a delightfully colorful, wickedly eccentric Romanian gentleman and scholar, interspersed with advice and inspiration for Alzheimer's caregivers everywhere. In addition to being a compelling love story, this award-winning memoir shows how one woman solved several problems that typically arise when caring for a person with dementia.

Finding Joy in Alzheimer's: New Hope for Caregivers (2015)

Available in paperback and Kindle version

Here's a hope-filled book about Alzheimer's caregiving. This groundbreaking volume will give you hope in the midst of the darkness of Alzheimer's and other dementias. You can come to terms with your loved one's condition and free yourself tao experience joyous interactions.

“Finding Joy in Alzheimer's” is a “How To” book for Alzheimer's caregivers, written with neurologist co-author, Daniel Potts, MD, FAAN. The Foreword was written by Maria Shriver, whose father, Sargeant Shriver, had Alzheimer's.

The Concerto: A Novel (2026)

Available in paperback and Kindle version

“The Concerto” tells the tale Melody Rosen, a world-famous flute player, who falls in love with and marries a plumber. Her primary goal in life is to find unconditional love.

Melody and her plumber husband, Paul Rosen, have a delightful life together and Melody is convinced she has long last found that kind of love.

But after she develops Alzheimer's, life goes steadily downhill.

Silent Witness to Murder

Coming 2027

The last person social worker, Janice Jameson, expects to see her first day on the job—her first job out of college—is the nursing home's elderly medical director, Marvin Donaldson. He's dead.

“Silent Witness to Murder,” coming in July 2007, is a mystery about a young social worker, Janice Jameson, turned amateur sleuth. To solve the crime, she must develop a bond and learn to communicate nonverbally with Nancy Longarden, a nursing home resident who is the only witness to the murder. Nancy has frontotemporal dementia, which has rendered her unable to speak.
