Come Back Early Today

By Marie Marley, PhD

An intimate memoir of a decade spent as the primary caregiver for Eduard, the love of Marie’s life, as he disappeared into Alzheimer’s disease. Written with wit, sorrow, and extraordinary compassion, Come Back Early Today is the book countless caregivers have called their lifeline.

About the Book

When Eduard began to forget, Marie refused to. Come Back Early Today is the account of ten years spent navigating the Alzheimer’s care system, fighting for dignity on behalf of someone who could no longer fight for himself, and discovering — again and again — that love does not require recognition to remain real.

Marie writes about the practical and the profound: negotiating with nursing homes, finding translators for a mind that had forgotten English and retreated into Czech, singing songs Eduard remembered even after he had forgotten her name. She writes about the guilt of going to dinner, the relief of a good day, and the strange gift of being present for someone else’s disappearance.

Grounded in her own lived experience and enriched by her background in human development, Marie also offers practical wisdom woven through the memoir: strategies for difficult conversations, approaches to communicating with someone in cognitive decline, and the importance of caregiver self-care that so often gets sacrificed in the service of someone else’s survival.

This book will not fix Alzheimer’s. But it will make anyone who reads it feel less alone. That is, perhaps, the only promise a book about this disease is allowed to make — and Marie Marley keeps it.
