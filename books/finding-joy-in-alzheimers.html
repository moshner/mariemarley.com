About the Book

Here's a hope-filled book about Alzheimer's caregiving. This groundbreaking volume will give you hope in the midst of the darkness of Alzheimer's and other dementias. You can come to terms with your loved one's condition and free yourself tao experience joyous interactions.

“Finding Joy in Alzheimer's” is a “How To” book for Alzheimer's caregivers, written with neurologist co-author, Daniel Potts, MD, FAAN. The Foreword was written by Maria Shriver, whose father, Sargeant Shriver, had Alzheimer's.

Part I covers a variety of issues, such as the authors' belief that people with Alzheimer's can still enjoy life, how to overcome denial, handling five especially difficult situations, the role of grief on the journey to acceptance, and letting go of resentment.

In Part II the authors provide 55 helpful tips for visiting people with Alzheimer's.

Part III consists of numerous short stories illustrating the authors' joyous interactions with their loved ones. The stories will warm your heart and light your way along the path to achieving true joy.
