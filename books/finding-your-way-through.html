About the Book

The Concerto began as a response to the questions Marie Marley was asked after Come Back Early Today was published. Readers wanted to know: How do you talk to someone with Alzheimer’s when they no longer recognize you? How do you choose a care facility without being eaten alive by guilt? How do you grieve someone who is still alive? How do you take care of yourself when taking care of yourself feels like a betrayal?

This book is Marie’s attempt to answer those questions honestly. Drawing on her decade of experience, her doctoral work in human development, and the hundreds of caregiver conversations she has had since, she offers clear, practical guidance alongside the emotional context that makes guidance actually usable.

Chapters address: navigating the healthcare system, handling difficult family dynamics around caregiving, understanding behavioral changes in Alzheimer’s and how to respond, making end-of-life decisions with care and clarity, and building a sustainable caregiving life that does not require the destruction of your own health and relationships.

This is not a book that tells you caregiving is beautiful (though it sometimes is). It is a book that tells you it is survivable, and gives you the tools to survive it without losing yourself entirely.
