About the Book

The last person social worker, Janice Jameson, expects to see her first day on the job—her first job out of college—is the nursing home's medical director, Marvin Donaldson. He's dead.

“Silent Witness to Murder,” coming in 2027, is a mystery about a young social worker, Janice Jameson, turned amateur sleuth. To solve the crime, she must develop a bond and learn to communicate nonverbally with Nancy Longarden, a nursing home resident who is the only witness to the murder. Nancy has frontotemporal dementia, which has rendered her unable to speak.

Several people have motives to kill Donaldson, and Janice receives messages warning her to get off the case if she values her life. But she persists, and eventually Nancy finds a way to reveal the murderer's initials.

Then late one evening, as a violent storm rages and a tornado watch is issued, Jan finds herself alone in the east wing of the nursing home, face to face with two men who have those initials. Jan wonders which one is the killer. And whether he is going to strangle her.
