About the Book

"Silent Witness to Murder" is a gripping mystery that follows Janice Jameson, a newly hired nursing home social worker whose first day on the job begins with a shocking discovery: the facility's medical director has been murdered.

As Janice searches for answers, she forms an unlikely connection with Nancy Longarden, a resident with frontotemporal dementia who cannot speak but may hold the key to solving the crime. With suspects mounting and anonymous threats warning her to walk away, Janice is drawn deeper into a dangerous investigation.

Set against the unique backdrop of a nursing home, "Silent Witness to Murder" is a suspenseful story of courage, compassion, and the determination to uncover the truth before it's too late.
