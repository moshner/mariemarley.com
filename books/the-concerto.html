The Concerto

By Marie Marley, PhD

“The Concerto” tells the tale Melody Rosen, a world-famous flute player, who falls in love with and marries a plumber. Her primary goal in life is to find unconditional love.

Melody and her plumber husband, Paul Rosen, have a delightful life together and Melody is convinced she has long last found that kind of love.

But after she develops Alzheimer's, life goes steadily downhill.

About the Book

"The Concerto" is Marie Marley's debut literary novel about Melody Rosen, a world-renowned flutist whose life is defined by extraordinary musical talent and an enduring search for unconditional love. When she unexpectedly finds happiness with a plumber ten years her junior who changes her life, it seems she has finally discovered everything she's been longing for.

But as Alzheimer's disease begins to reshape her world, Melody must confront devastating losses, difficult choices, and the question of what gives life meaning when so much has been taken away.

A poignant and deeply human story of love, resilience, and hope, "The Concerto" explores whether the heart can still find what it longs for, even in life's darkest moments.

What Readers Are Saying

“Marie Marley's debut literary novel, 'The Concerto', is an engrossing story of loss and love in which Melody, a world-famous flutist, struggles with multiple losses that develop rapidly due to her advancing Alzheimer's. Anger, denial and fractured relationships keep Melody in a whirlwind of grief and confusion. Her illness threatens her marriage while it strengthens the bond with her daughter, Charity, who rises to the occasion to comfort and assist Melody, overcoming their earlier estrangement. Hoping that Melody will conquer her depression and resistance to help, and perhaps even find joy, keeps the reader turning the pages to the very end.”

— Jean Youkers, author of "Interlude at Rehoboth"
