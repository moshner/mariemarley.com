About the Book

“The Concerto” tells the tale Melody Rosen, a world-famous flute player, who falls in love with and marries a plumber. Her primary goal in life is to find unconditional love.

Melody and her plumber husband, Paul Rosen, have a delightful life together and Melody is convinced she has long last found that kind of love.

But after she develops Alzheimer's, life goes steadily downhill.

First, she's fired from the orchestra for making too many mistakes. Then Paul leaves her because seeing his wife's mind disintegrate before his very eyes is just too upsetting. After that Melody has to euthanize her dearly beloved Shih Tzu, Andrew.

Finally, Melody, who has become clinically depressed, decides to take her own life. It seems she won't live to achieve her goal.
